The process

Four steps from bare dirt to buildable plan

The order matters: we learn the site, then your taste, then design, then hand it to someone who can build it.

Step 01

Film your yard

Walk the property with your phone using our Yard Builder prompts. Add the lot survey, sun exposure, drainage headaches, and the budget you're working with.

Step 02

Pick your palette

Swipe through hardscape materials, furniture, and plants pre-filtered to your USDA zone, water restrictions, and how much maintenance you actually want to do.

Step 03

Meet your design team

A licensed landscape designer and a regional horticulturist build your plan, then walk you through the 3D renderings live. One full revision round is included.

Step 04

Build it with a vetted crew

We match you with licensed, insured contractors in your area, share the plan set with them, and compare their bids with you line by line before you sign anything.

What you get

A plan set a contractor can bid from

Not a mood board. Every Yardline design ships as a package your crew, your city permit office, and your nursery can all work from.

  • Photorealistic 3D renderings

    Your actual house and lot, rendered from the angles you stand in most — plus a dusk view so you can see the lighting.

  • Scaled site plan & CAD files

    Dimensioned layout with material callouts, square footages, and DWG/PDF exports for your builder.

  • Planting plan & shoppable list

    Species, quantities, and container sizes — with a drought-tolerant substitute named for every plant, in case your nursery is out.

  • Cost bands & build phasing

    A realistic install range for your region, broken into phases so you can build the patio this year and the pool next.
